Website Privacy & Cookie Notice

Effective Date: 4 June 2026

This notice explains how the DailyBliss website (dailybliss.github.io) handles data and cookies. It is separate from the privacy policies of our individual apps, which are linked at the end.

1. Who this applies to

This notice covers the DailyBliss website only. Each DailyBliss app has its own privacy policy describing how that app handles data on your device. Links to those are at the bottom of this page.

2. What the website collects

The DailyBliss website does not ask you to create an account, and it has no contact forms, logins, or newsletter sign-ups. We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number through this website.

The website does use Google Ads (gtag.js) for advertising measurement and to understand whether our ad campaigns are effective — for example, whether visitors who arrive from an ad go on to click through to the App Store or Google Play. To do this, Google Ads may set cookies and process limited data such as:

  • Cookies and similar identifiers used for advertising measurement and remarketing.
  • Technical information such as your approximate location (derived from IP address), device and browser type, and the pages you visit on this site.
  • Whether you clicked a tracked link, such as the "Download on the App Store" or "Get it on Google Play" button.

This data may be used by Google for both personalized and non-personalized advertising, and access to it may be available to Google as a third party. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent (EEA, UK & Switzerland)

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, advertising and measurement cookies are not set until you give consent. When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept or decline. Until you choose "Accept", Google's tags are set to a denied consent state and do not store advertising cookies or use your data for personalization.

Visitors outside these regions are not shown the banner, and standard advertising measurement applies.

4. Managing or withdrawing your consent

You can change your choice at any time — withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it. To do so, clear this site's cookies in your browser settings; the consent banner will then appear again on your next visit, letting you make a new choice.

You can also control ad personalization across Google services at any time through Google My Ad Center.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, trade, or rent any information.
  • We do not run any analytics beyond the Google Ads measurement described above.
  • We do not use the website to build profiles about you for our own purposes.

6. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
