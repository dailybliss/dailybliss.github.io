10 Best Mantra Counter Apps (2026)

Compared on downloads, ratings, platform, pricing, and the features that actually matter for daily japa.

A mantra counter app replaces the traditional 108-bead mala with your phone — tap to count, track your rounds, and watch your practice build over time. There are hundreds of options, so we compared the best ones on the things that matter: how many people use them, how they're rated, which platforms they support, what they cost, and the standout features. Here are the 10 best mantra counter apps in 2026, plus a free online counter.

1. MantraCounter by DailyBliss

Platform: Android & iOS · Rating: 4.8 · Price: 30-day free trial, then subscription

MantraCounter is built for serious daily practice rather than just tapping a number. You enroll each mantra with its own name, deity image, daily target, and lifetime goal, then track every session — with streaks, totals, and progress toward long-term goals like a 1,25,000-count purashcharana. On Android it offers an auto counting mode that estimates your pace hands-free once it learns your rhythm (manual tap mode stays available for exact counts), and the Android version is available in 25+ languages, switching automatically to your phone's language. Everything works offline, and you can export a full JSON backup at any time.

  • Standout features: mantra enrollments, daily + lifetime goals, streaks & statistics, auto count (Android), 25+ languages (Android), backup & restore, fully offline.
  • App link: Google Play · App Store
  • Website: dailybliss.app/apps/mantracounter

2. Mantra Counter (by Davda)

Platform: Android · Rating: 4.6 · Price: Free with ads (ad-free upgrade available)

It keeps things simple and personal: set your own deity photo as the background, choose background music, pick from six themes (including a dark mode), and enter mantras as text. A "No God Display" mode lets you count without a deity image. Vibration feedback confirms each tap.

  • Standout features: deity background, custom music, six themes, vibration feedback.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it okay to count mantras on a phone instead of a mala?

Yes. The purpose of counting is to keep the mind focused and track repetitions accurately — a digital counter does this just as well as beads. Many practitioners use a physical mala for seated practice and an app for tracking totals and streaks over time.

What is auto counting mode?

Some apps can count repetitions automatically once they've learned your chanting pace, so you don't have to tap each time. It gives a reliable estimate for hands-free practice; for an exact count, manual tap mode is best.

Are mantra counter apps free?

Most are free with ads. Some offer ad-free upgrades or premium features via in-app purchase, and a few (like MantraCounter) use a free trial followed by a subscription for their full tracking features. Free online counters need no download at all.

Which mantra counter works on iPhone?

MantraCounter and Mantra Repeater are both available on iOS. Many counters are Android-only, so iPhone users have fewer options — check the App Store link before installing.
