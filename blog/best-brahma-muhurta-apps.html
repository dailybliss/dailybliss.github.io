5 Best Brahma Muhurta Time Apps

Compared on ratings, platform, widgets, alarms, location accuracy, and pricing.

Brahma Muhurta — the sacred window of roughly 48 minutes before sunrise — is considered the most auspicious time for meditation and spiritual practice. But because it's tied to sunrise, it shifts a little every day and depends on your exact location, so a fixed time never quite fits. A good Brahma Muhurta app calculates the window for you automatically — so you know exactly when it falls, whether you wake naturally or set a gentle reminder. Here are the 5 best Brahma Muhurta time apps, plus a free online tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the Brahma Muhurta time change every day?

Because it's tied to sunrise, which shifts slightly each day with the earth's orbit and your latitude — and it differs by location. That's why a dedicated app (or tool) that recalculates automatically is more accurate than a fixed time.

Do these apps need an internet connection?

Most calculate the window on your device and work offline once installed. Some need location access (GPS) to pinpoint your coordinates; a few let you set a city manually instead.

Which Brahma Muhurta app works on iPhone?

SereneRise, Brahma: Brahmamuhurta Alarm, and Brahma Muhurat are available on iOS. Many apps in this category are Android-only, so iPhone users have fewer choices — check the App Store link before installing.

What's the difference between a Brahma Muhurta widget and an alarm?

A widget shows the time on your home screen so you can glance at it; an alarm wakes you up at (or before) the window. If you simply want to know the time, a widget app like SereneRise is ideal; if you need to be woken, choose an app with a reliable alarm — but test it first, as alarm reliability varies by device and battery settings.

Should I use an alarm to wake up for Brahma Muhurta?

Traditionally, it's recommended to rise naturally during Brahma Muhurta rather than being jolted awake by an alarm — waking gently, on your own, is considered part of the calm the practice is meant to cultivate. Going to bed earlier is the surest way to wake in time. If you do rely on an alarm while building the habit, make sure it rings consistently, and aim to need it less over time. Knowing your exact window (via a widget or tool) and winding down early is often enough.
