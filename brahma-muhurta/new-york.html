What is Brahma Muhurta?

Brahma Muhurta is the sacred pre-dawn window traditionally reserved for meditation, yoga, and spiritual practice. The name means "the time of Brahma" or "the hour of the Creator." It begins roughly 1 hour 36 minutes before sunrise and lasts about 48 minutes — one muhurta in the traditional division of the day into thirty muhurtas of 48 minutes each.

New York is home to one of the largest Indian-American communities in the United States, concentrated in Queens, Jersey City, and Long Island, and many here keep the practice of rising in Brahma Muhurta for meditation and yoga — a tradition carried from India and adapted to life in New York.

Why Practise in Brahma Muhurta?

The window is prized because the mind is naturally calm after sleep, the city is quiet before the day begins, and tradition holds that clarity (sattva) is at its peak. For anyone in New York looking to begin a meditation or yoga practice, the Brahma Muhurta window offers the stillest, most undisturbed time of day.
