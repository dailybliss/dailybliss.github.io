Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 20, 2026

SereneRise is built with your privacy as a priority. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. About SereneRise

SereneRise is a Brahma Muhurta time calculator app developed by DailyBliss. It calculates the auspicious pre-sunrise period based on your device's location using the NOAA Solar Calculator algorithm. The app is available on iOS and Android.

2. Information We Collect

SereneRise collects minimal information necessary to provide its core functionality:

  • Location data — Your device's GPS coordinates are used solely to calculate accurate sunrise and Brahma Muhurta times for your area. Location data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.
  • No account information — SereneRise does not require you to create an account. No name, email, or personal details are collected.
  • No analytics — We do not use any analytics SDKs or tracking tools.
  • No advertising — SereneRise contains no advertisements and no advertising identifiers are collected.

3. How We Use Your Location

Your location is used exclusively for:

  • Calculating the precise sunrise time for your area
  • Determining your daily Brahma Muhurta time window
  • Updating the home screen widget with accurate times

Your location coordinates are stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. They are never uploaded to any server, never shared with third parties, and never used for any purpose other than time calculation.

4. Location Permission

SereneRise requests access to your device's location when you use the app. You can control location permissions at any time through your device's Settings app. If you deny location permission, the app will be unable to calculate accurate times for your area.

On iOS, SereneRise requests "When In Use" location permission — meaning location is only accessed when the app is open. On Android, you may optionally grant background location permission to allow automatic updates when your location changes significantly.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

SereneRise offers an optional premium subscription. All payments are processed securely by the platform's payment system — Apple App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. DailyBliss does not collect, store, or have access to your payment information, credit card details, or billing address.

Subscription management, renewals, and cancellations are handled entirely by the respective platform. Please refer to Apple's or Google's privacy policies for details on how payment data is handled.

6. Data Storage

All data used by SereneRise — including your location coordinates, subscription status, and app preferences — is stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to or stored on DailyBliss servers. If you delete the app, all associated data is permanently removed from your device.

7. Third-Party Services

SereneRise does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only third-party interaction is with the platform's payment system (Apple App Store or Google Play) for subscription processing.

8. Children's Privacy

SereneRise is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app is rated 4+ on the App Store and carries an equivalent rating on Google Play.

9. Data Security

Since SereneRise stores all data locally on your device, the security of your data is protected by your device's built-in security features including encryption and passcode/biometric protection. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date for the best security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of SereneRise after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
