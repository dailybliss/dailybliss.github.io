About This Mantra Library

Each entry gives the mantra in Devanagari and in transliteration, a short guide to pronouncing it, its meaning, and the benefits it is traditionally chanted for. Pronunciations are approximate guides to the Sanskrit and Gurmukhi sounds — listen to a teacher where you can, and chant slowly enough to keep the long and short vowels distinct.

Use the search box to find a mantra by name, sound or meaning, the tradition chips to narrow the list, and the A–Z / By tradition toggle to reorder. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
